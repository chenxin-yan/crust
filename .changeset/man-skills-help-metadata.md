Man pages and skills use the new command metadata. Man pages gain an `EXAMPLES` section, list `.group()` commands and flags under `.Ss` subsections, show a flag's `valueName` as its `Ar` argument, and end a repeatable flag with `...`. Skill command files list `examples` under `## Examples` after the usage and show a flag's `valueName` with its last spelling.
